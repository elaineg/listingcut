## Clarity — Yes
Same 10-second pitch as last round: "Free site, no account, runs on your phone — cuts the
background off your product pic and spits out a white-background photo at eBay's exact size."
Headline + the eBay-guidelines line + the green "Your photo never leaves this device" pill
do all the work. The marketplace chips (eBay/Etsy/Poshmark/Depop/Facebook) instantly tell
you who it's for.

## Value — Marginal
Today I do nothing — dorm-floor pics, post as-is — so this is still an added step, not a
replaced tool. But it held up under real use: sample → clean cutout, one obvious blue
"Download white JPEG — 1600×1600 (eBay)" button, and the file actually came down named
`sample-mug-ebay-1600x1600.jpg`, ready to upload. Ran a second photo through and the cutout
was clean again. Two dings for my "list 6 pairs on Sunday" case:
- ~12s per photo even after the one-time download — 8 photos per pair adds up.
- Once your batch is done, the upload area is GONE. The only way to add a photo I forgot is
  "Start over," which wipes the results I just made. Let me add more without nuking the batch.

## Advocacy — 8
Still an 8, and that's a genuine 8 — if a friend's Jordans aren't moving I'd send him this
link, and I'd use it myself next batch day. What keeps it from 9:
- ~37s first run and ~12s per photo after; the page promises "after that it's fast" and it's
  only sort of fast. On dorm wifi I'd feel every one of those seconds.
- "Start over" being the only way to add another photo after a batch finishes — small thing,
  but it's exactly the move I'd hit mid-Sunday-listing-session.
- Ceiling is still me: it solves a problem I only half-believe I have. The eBay-guidelines
