## Prior concerns from round 1
1. ~18s first-run wait with no progress indicator — **mostly fixed.** Still took ~21s on
   the sample, but now I see why: "1. Downloading model (one-time, ~50 MB)" with a real
   percentage ticking up (33%… 95%), then "2. Removing background…" with a timer. The
   dropzone also warns upfront: "First photo downloads a one-time ~50 MB tool — after that
   it's fast." A moving percent bar is the difference between waiting and leaving. Same
   wait, but this time I didn't almost bail to TikTok.
2. No proof white backgrounds sell better — **partially fixed.** New line under the
   headline: "eBay & Poshmark feature white-background photos — clean listings sell
   faster." That's a reason, and it confirmed the TikTok thing for me. Still no number
   though — "sell faster" is a claim, not a stat.
3. Two confusing download buttons — **fixed.** Now there's one big blue "Download white
   JPEG — 1600×1600 (eBay)" and a clearly secondary "Download transparent PNG" with the
   caption "most marketplaces want the white JPEG above." Zero thinking required.

## Clarity — Yes
Same pitch I'd give my roommate: "Free site, runs on your phone, cuts the background off
your product pic and gives you a white-background photo at eBay's exact size. Nothing
uploads, no account." Headline + the eBay/Poshmark line + the green "Your photo never
leaves this device" pill do it in 10 seconds. StockX still isn't named, but I've accepted
this is for my eBay/Depop listings, and Depop is on the size chips now, which is where my
friends actually sell.

## Value — Marginal (but closer to Yes)
Today I do nothing — dorm-floor pics, post as-is. So this is still an added step, not a
replaced tool. But it's now cheap enough that I'd actually do it: the page says I can drop
up to 20 photos at once and they process one by one, which is exactly the "listing 6 pairs
on Sunday" case that killed it for me last round. One ~21s model download once, then batch
the rest. There's also a "Touch up" button if the auto-cutout misses an edge — didn't need
it on the sample (cutout was clean), but good to know it's there for laces/soles.

## Advocacy — 8
Last round I said "if the wait felt shorter and the page told me why white backgrounds
matter, this is an 8." They did both, so it's an 8. I'd genuinely show this to a friend
whose Jordans aren't moving, and I'd use it myself next time I batch-list. Why not 9–10:
- "clean listings sell faster" has no number behind it — one real stat would make me
  push this on people unprompted instead of just answering "what do you use?"
- 21 seconds is still 21 seconds the first time, even narrated well; on dorm wifi or
  LTE that ~50 MB download could be worse, and I only tried it on good wifi.
- It's still a tool for a problem I only half-believe I have; nothing here is wrong,
