# Panel round 3 — Tester 5 (Amara Singh, 24, Depop seller + barista, Pixel 7 / Chrome mobile)

## Prior concerns from round 2
1. **Preset chip looks like a download button; real button further down** — MOSTLY FIXED.
   The chips now live under a clear "Marketplace export — white background, exact size"
   header, tapping "Depop 1280×1280" instantly relabels the big button to "Download white
   JPEG — 1280×1280 (Depop)" and flips the live preview caption, AND every photo row in the
   queue has its own "Download" button that respects the selected preset (I tapped the row
   button and got exactly 1280×1280, named `tee-depop-1280x1280.jpg`). I never had to hunt
   for the big button this time. The chip itself still doesn't download, but with the row
   buttons I didn't care.
2. **Hero only named eBay & Poshmark** — FIXED. Subline now reads "...sized for eBay, Etsy,
   Poshmark, Depop, or Facebook — free, no upload, no signup." Depop sellers see themselves
   in the first 5 seconds now.

## Clarity — Yes
Friend pitch: "Free site — drop up to 20 product pics, it cuts the background and gives you
a white-background JPEG at the exact Depop size, nothing uploaded anywhere, no account."
Headline + the named marketplaces + the green "Your photo never leaves this device" pill do
all the work. Nothing confused me above the fold.

## Value — Yes
Today: Photoroom free tier (Pro nags, watermark pressure) or crop-and-hope. This still beats
it: batch queue, real Depop preset, no watermark, no signup, Touch up with Erase/Restore/Pan
that works with touch + pinch-zoom on my phone. New this round: when the auto-cutout comes
back nearly empty it flags the photo "Check this one — cutout looks nearly empty" and keeps
it out of the ZIP instead of silently handing me a blank square — two of my three test pics
got flagged and the advice ("Use Touch up → Restore to paint it back, or re-shoot with more
contrast") told me exactly what to do. Photoroom just gives you the bad cutout.

## Advocacy — 9
Already sent it to the Depop group chat after round 2; round 3 only made it tighter. Why
not 10: the first photo took ~46s on my run this time (round 2 was ~25s) — the "Downloading
model… 48%" progress bar kept me from bailing, but on cafe data that one-time wait is still
the moment a friend gives up before the magic happens. Also a small typo in the warning:
"1 photo ismarked "Check this one"" — missing space, looks sloppy in an otherwise clean app.
