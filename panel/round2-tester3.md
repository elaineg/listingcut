# Panel Round 2 — Tester 3 (Rita Vasquez, 41, Etsy ceramics shop, Windows/Edge)

## Prior concerns from round 1
1. **No batch upload — FIXED.** Drop zone now says "Drag & drop photos (up to 20)". I dropped
   2 photos at once, got a per-photo list with status, and "Download all (ZIP) — 2 photos,
   Etsy 2000×2000". The ZIP had both files, correctly named (vase-etsy-2000x2000.jpg), both
   verified real 2000×2000. This is exactly what I asked for.
2. **Preset reset to eBay each photo — FIXED.** One preset now applies to the whole batch,
   and after "Start over" with a new photo it stayed on Etsy 2000×2000. (A fresh tab still
   defaults to eBay, but one click per session is fine.)
3. **No margin/placement control — NOT FIXED.** Still no slider or nudge. My tall vase came
   out nearly touching the top and bottom of the square. The new "Touch up" (Erase/Restore
   brushes) is nice but it's for smudges, not framing.
4. **First-run model wait — PARTIALLY, and actually worse for me this time.** The drop zone
   now warns "First photo downloads a one-time ~50 MB tool — after that it's fast," which is
   honest. But on my actual first try BOTH photos failed with just "Failed to fetch" and no
   retry button — stuck at "Your photos (0/2 done)". I reloaded the page, re-uploaded, and
   then it worked in ~15 seconds. A first-timer might not reload; that error nearly lost me.

## Clarity — Yes
Same as round 1: "It cuts the background off your product photo and gives you white-background
JPEGs sized for Etsy, free, in your browser — now up to 20 at a time." The "(up to 20)" in the
drop zone and the ZIP button label made the batch story instantly clear.

## Value — Yes
Today: remove.bg free → Canva white background → Canva resize, ~4-5 min per photo, and my
12-photo listing session was the reason I held back last round. Now: drop 12, click Etsy once,
click "Download all (ZIP)". I also stress-tested edge quality with a speckled glaze vase on a
wood board — clean cutout, full 2000px (remove.bg free would have capped it). One nit: a faint
gray halo down one edge of the vase, fixable with the new Touch up eraser, invisible at
thumbnail size anyway. This genuinely replaces my three-tool flow.

## Advocacy — 8
The two things that held me at 8 last round are both fixed, and I will bring this up in my
Etsy seller Facebook group. What keeps it from a 9-10 is that my very first run this round
ended in "Failed to fetch" on both photos with no retry option — if a friend I recommended it
to hit that on her first try, she'd close the tab and I'd look silly. Add a "Retry" button on
failed photos (or auto-retry the model download) and a simple margin slider for tall pieces,
and this is a 9-10 I'd push on people unprompted.
