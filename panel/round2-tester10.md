# Sam — Round 2

PM, mobile between meetings. I drop a product shot/screenshot onto a clean white or brand
background to polish a stakeholder deck. Re-tested cold on a 375px phone, drove the whole
flow: uploaded a product photo, sat through the one-time ~50MB model download, got the
cutout, then exercised White export and Color export with Drop shadow ON/OFF.

## Re-check of my Round-1 complaints
- **Naming collision ("Shadow" vs "Remove shadow") — FIXED.** I now see "Remove cast
  shadow — Cleans the dark shadow under your subject" near the cutout, and "Drop shadow"
  in export. Two clearly different verbs; I no longer double-take about adding vs removing.
  The live download label is consistent too: "Download white JPEG (with drop shadow) —
  1080×1080 (Square)".
- **Drop shadow buried at bottom — MOSTLY FIXED.** The toggle now sits directly under
  Background (White/Color/Transparent) and the Margin slider — the three are grouped. It's
  no longer below a wall of controls; it's reachable and obviously part of the export step.
- **Long mobile scroll — PARTIALLY.** Better grouping, but the size grid (General & Social
  / Marketplaces) still sits ABOVE the Background/Margin/Drop-shadow block, so there's
  still a stretch of scroll between the cutout preview and the shadow toggle.

## 1. CLARITY — Yes
Unchanged and still the best cold-open I've tested. Headline + "Drop a photo and get a
clean cutout on white, your brand color, or transparent — free, no upload, no signup" plus
the "Headshots & avatars · ads & social · slides & mockups" chips name my exact job. The
green on-device pill answers my upload-a-not-yet-public-image worry.

## 2. VALUE — Yes
Today I bug a designer or fight remove.bg (nags me to pay for full-res + shadows) then
paste into Keynote. This does cutout + brand hex + deck/social preset sizes + a real drop
shadow in one no-login mobile screen. The naming fix matters: I can now confidently add a
shadow to ground the subject so it doesn't look pasted into the slide — that polish is the
thing I came for, and it's exactly what Photoroom paywalls.

## 3. ADVOCACY — 9 (R1 was 8)
Naming and placement are resolved, which clears my main hesitations, so I'd bring this up
unprompted in a #design-ops channel. Not a 10 because: (a) there's still no proactive nudge
near the preview that the drop-shadow feature exists — I only find it by scrolling into the
export panel, so a first-timer who came for "polish" could miss the headline feature; and
(b) on mobile the size grid sitting above Background/Margin/Drop-shadow still means a bit of
hunting between the cutout and the shadow control. Both are polish, not blockers.
