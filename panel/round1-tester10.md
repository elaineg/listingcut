# Round 1 — Tester 10: Helen Marsh (47, office manager, sells on Facebook Marketplace, Samsung A54 / Chrome)

## Clarity — Yes
I'd tell a friend: "You give it a photo of the thing you're selling and it cuts out the messy
background and puts it on white, like a real store listing. Free, nothing to sign up for."
The big headline "Listing-ready product photos in your browser" plus the before/after mug
pictures made it obvious in about ten seconds. The green note "Your photo never leaves this
device" was reassuring — I don't love uploading pictures of the inside of my house.
What confused me: it says "for eBay, Etsy, or Poshmark." I sell on Facebook Marketplace and
it's not mentioned anywhere, so right away I wondered if this was for me at all.

## What happened when I used it
- Tapped "Try the sample" — mug came out clean on white. Nice, I got it immediately.
- Uploaded my own couch photo. First it sat on "Downloading model (one-time, ~50 MB)."
  Fifty megabytes! On my phone plan that's not nothing. At least it told me, and it did finish.
- The couch came out on white — genuinely better than my crime-scene photo. BUT there were
  little leftover smudges of my living room floating around the couch, and I couldn't find any
  way to rub them out. So my "professional" photo still has confetti around it.
- The result screen pushes "Download PNG (transparent)" first with a checkerboard picture.
  I don't know what "transparent" means for a photo — checkerboard looks broken to me. The one
  I actually wanted (white background JPEG) was further down under "Marketplace export," and I
  had to pick eBay, Etsy, or Poshmark sizes. I just picked eBay and hoped Facebook wouldn't mind.
- The "Live preview" box showed up as an empty grey square on my phone, but the downloaded
  picture was fine. Confusing for a moment — I thought it had failed.

## Value — Marginal
Today I do nothing: I post straight from my camera roll to Facebook Marketplace, and Facebook
doesn't require white backgrounds. So this is a nice-to-have, not a need. It did make my couch
photo look more "shop-like" in about a minute, which is more than I'd get from any app I'd
bother to learn. But the leftover smudges mean I still might not post it, and re-finding the
right download button each time is friction. If it said "Facebook Marketplace" and had a little
eraser for the leftover bits, this would tip into a real Yes.

## Advocacy — 5
I'd mention it if a friend complained about ugly listing photos, but I wouldn't bring it up on
my own. Held back by: (1) no Facebook Marketplace option — the one place I sell; (2) leftover
background smudges with no way to clean them; (3) "Download PNG (transparent)" being the big
button when the white-background JPEG is what people like me want; (4) the ~50 MB first-time
download — on a cheaper phone or thin data plan I'd have given up.
