# Sam — Round 1

I'm a PM, mobile between meetings. A few times a month I drop a product shot or screenshot
onto a clean white or brand background to make a stakeholder deck look sharp. Tested on a
375px phone viewport, cold.

## 1. CLARITY — Yes
Within 5 seconds I knew exactly what it is and that it's for me. The headline "Remove any
background — keep your photo on this device" plus "Drop a photo and get a clean cutout on
white, your brand color, or transparent — free, no upload, no signup" is dead-on. The use
chips ("slides & mockups", "ads & social") literally name my job. The green "Your photos
never leave this device" pill answered the question I'd have asked before uploading a
not-yet-public product image. No jargon, nothing to figure out.

## 2. VALUE — Yes
Today I either bug a designer on Slack or wrestle with remove.bg (which nags me to sign up /
pay for full-res and for shadows) and then paste into Keynote. This did the whole thing in
one screen, on my phone, no login: cutout, pick White or a brand hex (#1d4ed8 worked, and
there's a row of preset swatches + a hex box), set margin, and a one-time ~50MB model so
later photos are fast. The preset sizes ("Slide 16:9 1920×1080", "Story", "Link/Ad") mean I
don't resize afterward — that's the part that actually saves me time vs my current habit.
The new **Shadow** toggle (Soft/Medium/Strong) is exactly the thing Photoroom paywalls; in
the live preview it grounds the subject so it doesn't look pasted-on. Genuinely makes a deck
image look more deliberate.

## 3. ADVOCACY — 8
I'd recommend it, and I'd probably drop the link in a #design-ops Slack channel. Two things
keep it off a 9–10 for me:
- The **Shadow** toggle is a touch buried. It lives at the very bottom of the long "Export
  background" panel, below the size grid and margin slider, with no hint up top that the
  new shadow feature exists. As the guy who came for "polish my deck image," I almost missed
  the headline feature. A small "New: drop shadow" nudge near the preview would land it.
- There are TWO shadow things and they confused me for a second: "Remove shadow (auto-cleans
  cast shadows from cutouts)" near the cutout, and "Shadow" (add one) in export. Reasonable,
  but the wording collision made me double-take about whether I was adding or removing.

## What worked
- Cold-open clarity is the best I've seen on this panel-type tool; copy names my exact job.
- Download button label updates live: "Download JPEG on this background (with shadow) —
  1080×1080 (Square) · beige" — I know precisely what I'm getting before I tap.
- Brand color via hex + swatches + preset deck/social sizes = no post-edit resizing.
- No login, on-device, fast after first model load. Export filename was tidy too.

## What held me back
- Shadow feature isn't discoverable for someone who came specifically for it.
- "Remove shadow" vs "Shadow" wording is briefly ambiguous.
- Long single-column scroll on mobile between the result and the export controls; I scrolled
  past the preview a couple times hunting for the right control.
