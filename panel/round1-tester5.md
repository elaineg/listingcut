# Panel round 1 — Tester 5 (Amara Singh, 24, Depop seller + barista, Pixel 7 / Chrome mobile)

## Clarity — Yes
In the first screen I'd tell a friend: "It's a free site that cuts your product photo off
its background and gives you a white-background pic sized for eBay/Etsy/Poshmark, no
account needed." The headline "Listing-ready product photos in your browser" plus the
subline "free, no upload, no signup" did all the work. The green "Your photo never leaves
this device" badge actually made me trust it more, not less. Nothing above the fold
confused me. One miss: it says eBay, Etsy, Poshmark — I sell on Depop, and Depop is not
named anywhere, so for a second I wondered if it's "for me."

## Value — Yes
Today I use Photoroom free tier, which keeps nudging me to Pro, or I just crop tight and
hope. This did the whole job on my phone with zero signup: I uploaded a red sneaker shot
on a RED background (a nightmare case) and the cutout came back clean — laces, swoosh,
everything. The eBay/Etsy/Poshmark buttons give an exact-size white JPEG, and the
download was literally `shoe-poshmark-1200x1200.jpg` at exactly 1200x1200. That's the
big-seller look I wanted, free.

Honest friction:
- First photo took ~40s because it downloads a ~50 MB model. The progress bar with "%
  one-time download" copy is the ONLY reason I didn't bail — but 50 MB on phone data is
  a real cost they should warn about before I tap upload, not after.
- Every later photo still took ~12s on this machine; on my actual Pixel mid-shift at the
  cafe that's probably longer. There IS a progress indicator, so I'd tolerate it, barely.
- No Depop preset. Depop wants 1280×1280 square; I used "Poshmark square" as a stand-in
  and it worked, but I shouldn't have to guess that. It's one button.

## Advocacy — 8
I would genuinely show this to the girls in my Depop group chat — "free background
remover, no watermark, no signup" sells itself. What keeps it from a 9–10:
1. No Depop preset (quote the page: "eBay 1600×1600 / Etsy 2000×2000 / Poshmark square"
   — where's Depop?). I'd recommend it with an asterisk.
2. The 50 MB model download isn't disclosed until AFTER you upload ("Downloading model
   (one-time, ~50 MB)"). On mobile data that's a trust ding.
3. ~12s per photo even after the first; batch sellers shooting 10 items will feel it,
   and there's no multi-photo queue.
