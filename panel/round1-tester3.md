# Panel Round 1 — Tester 3 (Rita Vasquez, 41, Etsy ceramics shop, Windows/Edge)

## Clarity — Yes
Within 10 seconds I could tell a friend: "It cuts the background off your product photo and
gives you a white-background JPEG already sized for Etsy or eBay, free, no signup." The
subtitle literally says "white-background JPEG sized for eBay, Etsy, or Poshmark" — those
are my words. The before/after mug picture in the drop zone and "Try the sample" made it
obvious. The green "Your photo never leaves this device" badge actually mattered to me —
I've wondered where remove.bg sends my photos.

## Value — Yes
Today: remove.bg free (annoying resolution cap) → Canva to put on white → Canva resize to
2000px. Three tools, maybe 4-5 minutes a photo. Here I dropped my mug photo, clicked
"Etsy 2000×2000", clicked "Download 2000×2000 JPEG" — done. I verified the file: real
2000x2000 JPEG, clean white background, product nicely centered with margin. Big deal for
me: my 1200px photo came out at full 2000px — remove.bg free would have capped it tiny and
I'd be upscaling a mushy image in Canva. The transparent PNG download is a bonus for
collages. This replaces my whole flow for thumbnails.

Gripes that cost it points:
1. **First run is slow and a little scary.** "Downloading model (one-time, ~50 MB)" then
   "Removing background… 8s" — about 30 seconds before I saw anything. The message does
   explain it's one-time, which kept me from closing the tab, but on my older laptop and
   shop wifi 50 MB is a real wait.
2. **One photo at a time.** I shoot 10-15 listings in a batch. No multi-upload, and after
   each photo I have to "Start over" and re-pick the preset (it reset to eBay 1600x1600 —
   I almost downloaded the wrong size; the Etsy button I'd want remembered).
3. **No control over placement/margin.** Canva let me nudge the mug or zoom it. Here I get
   whatever centering it picks. It picked fine for me, but I'd want a margin slider for
   tall vases.
4. Untested worry: my real photos have textured glazes against wood boards; my test image
   had clean edges, so I can't swear by edge quality on tricky shots.

## Advocacy — 8
I would actually mention this in my Etsy seller Facebook group — "free remove.bg with no
resolution cap that also sizes for Etsy" sells itself. It's not a 9-10 because batch
upload is missing (my real session is 12 photos, so the per-photo clicking adds up) and
the preset defaulting back to "eBay 1600×1600" after each photo is a trap for an Etsy
seller.
