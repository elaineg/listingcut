# Round 1 — Tester 9: Caleb Wright (19, sneaker flipper, iPhone 15 Safari)

## Clarity — Yes
30 seconds in I could tell my roommate: "It's a free site that cuts the background out of
your product pic and spits out a white-background photo sized for eBay. No account, nothing
gets uploaded." The headline "Listing-ready product photos in your browser" plus the
sub-line naming eBay/Etsy/Poshmark did all the work. The green "Your photo never leaves
this device" pill actually registered — I don't love uploading pics of my inventory to
random sites. Small gripe: "Poshmark square" — I don't sell on Poshmark, and StockX (where
half my pairs go) isn't mentioned, which made me wonder for a second if this was "for me."
(StockX uses stock photos anyway, so this is really for my eBay listings.)

## Value — Marginal
Today I do nothing — I shoot on my dorm floor and post as-is, and stuff still sells. So
this isn't replacing a tool, it's asking me to add a step. That said, it's about the
lowest-friction version of that step I've seen: I tapped "Try the sample," then dropped my
own photo, and got a clean white-bg 1600x1600 JPEG with the carpet gone — two taps, no
signup, no watermark, free. The catch: the first run took ~18 seconds with just "Working on
your photo… First run takes longer" on screen. Eighteen seconds is forever on a phone; I
almost backed out to TikTok. If I'm listing 6 pairs, that wait per photo (even if later
runs are faster, I didn't know that) is the difference between "sure" and "nah."
Filename `sneaker-ebay-1600x1600.jpg` ready to attach to a listing was a nice touch.

## Advocacy — 6
I'd show it to a friend if we were literally talking about why his Jordans aren't selling,
but I wouldn't bring it up on my own. What holds it back:
1. The ~18s first-run wait with only "First run takes longer while the model loads" —
   no progress bar, no countdown. I genuinely almost left.
2. It's solving a problem I'm not convinced I have. Nothing on the page backs the TikTok
   claim — even one line like "white backgrounds get X% more clicks on eBay" would have
   converted me from curious to sold.
3. Two download buttons ("Download PNG (transparent)" and "Download 1600x1600 JPEG") —
   I had to think about which one eBay wants; just tell me.
What it did right: the sample demo means zero commitment, the cutout was actually clean,
and free/no-signup/no-upload removes every excuse. If the wait felt shorter and the page
told me why white backgrounds matter, this is an 8 for someone like me.
