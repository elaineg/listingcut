# Panel round 2 — Tester 5 (Amara Singh, 24, Depop seller + barista, Pixel 7 / Chrome mobile)

## Prior concerns from round 1 — all three fixed
1. **No Depop preset** — FIXED. There's now a "Depop 1280×1280" chip right between Poshmark
   and Facebook. I tapped it, got a live preview, and the download was literally
   `tee-depop-1280x1280.jpg` at exactly 1280×1280. No more guessing with Poshmark square.
2. **50 MB download only disclosed after upload** — FIXED. The dropzone itself now says
   "First photo downloads a one-time ~50 MB tool — after that it's fast." I read that
   BEFORE picking a photo, so no mobile-data ambush. That's exactly what I asked for.
3. **No batch / ~12s per photo** — FIXED. "Drag & drop photos (up to 20)" with a queue
   ("Your photos (1/3 done)"), per-photo Download buttons, and a Download all. I ran 3
   photos: after the model warmed up they finished roughly 7s, then 2s apart — way better
   than round 1, and I can leave it chewing through a haul while I steam milk.

## Clarity — Yes
Same pitch I'd give a friend: "Free site that cuts the background off your product pics
and spits out a white-background JPEG at the exact size your marketplace wants — no
signup, photos never leave your phone." Headline + "free, no upload, no signup" still do
the work. Tiny note: the hero subline still says "eBay & Poshmark feature white-background
photos" — Depop isn't name-dropped until the preset chips, so a Depop seller skimming the
top might not realize it's for them. The chip saved it.

## Value — Yes
Today: Photoroom free tier (constant Pro nags) or crop-and-hope. This now beats it
outright for me: batch up to 20, a real Depop preset, no watermark, no account, and a new
"Touch up" button for fixing edges the auto-cutout misses. The 1/N done counter means I
never sat staring at an unexplained spinner. Photoroom gives me nothing this doesn't,
and this doesn't nag me.

I'd bring this up in the Depop group chat unprompted — actually already drafted the
message in my head: "free bg remover, has a Depop size button, no watermark." Why not 10:
first-photo wait is still real (~25s total on good wifi, longer on cafe data), and on
