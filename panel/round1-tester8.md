# Round 1 — Tester 8 (Priya Patel, Shopify/eBay seller, Windows laptop + Chrome)

## Clarity — Yes
Within 10 seconds I'd tell a friend: "It cuts the background off your product photo and gives
you a white-background JPEG already sized for eBay or Etsy, free, right in the browser."
What helped: the headline "Listing-ready product photos in your browser", the before/after mug
graphic, and especially "free, no upload, no signup" — that answered my first three questions.
"Try the sample" was a smart touch; I clicked it and saw the whole flow before risking my own photo.

## Value — Yes (with one big asterisk)
Today I pay a Fiverr freelancer $15 per batch of 20 photos and wait 2 days. I uploaded my own
phone-case photo: first run took ~42 seconds (it tells you "First run takes longer while the
model downloads; next photos are fast" — honest, and the numbered progress steps kept me from
bailing). The eBay export downloaded as a real 1600×1600 white-background JPEG with a sensible
filename (product0-ebay-1600x1600.jpg). That's same-day listing instead of 2 days. Real value.

The asterisk, in order of pain:
1. **No batch upload.** The file picker only takes ONE photo at a time. My unit of work is 20
   photos. One-at-a-time means 20 rounds of upload → wait → click "Download 1600×1600 JPEG" →
   "Start over". Still beats 2 days and $15, but this is exactly where I'd quit and go back to
   Fiverr on a busy week. Even just multi-select + a "download all" zip would change my life.
2. **The cutout kept the shadow.** My photo had a soft shadow under the case; the "cutout" kept
   that brown shadow blob, so my "white background" eBay image has a dirty smudge under the
   product. My Fiverr guy removes shadows. I'd have to re-shoot or fix these by hand, which
   means I'd still inspect every single image.
3. Presets are eBay/Etsy/Poshmark. I sell on Shopify + eBay — eBay square works for Shopify
   too, so livable, but "Shopify" or a custom-size option would feel like it's for me.

## Advocacy — 6
I would tell another reseller "there's a free browser thing that does white-background cutouts
instantly" — but with the caveat "one photo at a time, and check the shadows." For a one-off
urgent listing it's a clear win over waiting on Fiverr. For my actual 20-photo batches it's a
chore, and the shadow-blob output means I can't trust it unsupervised. Batch upload + cleaner
shadow handling and this is an 8–9 I'd bring up unprompted in my reseller Facebook group.
