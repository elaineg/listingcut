# ListingCut
Purpose: for anyone who needs a clean cutout — headshots/avatars, ads & social, slides & mockups, and marketplace listings — remove a photo's background entirely in the browser, then one-click export a JPEG on white, a brand color, or transparent at any preset size.
Problem: An Etsy/eBay/Poshmark seller prepping product listing photos, a job-seeker making a headshot, or anyone making a sticker/meme/slide needs a subject cut out of a photo — common one-off task, ~1–5 images per session, weekly-to-monthly for the typical user (daily only for professional sellers).
Beats alternative: Free client-side removers (NukeBG, bg.addy.ie) stop at a transparent PNG; the few that offer a colored background (addyosmani/bg-remove, Sticker Mule Trace) export at the photo's original size, not a marketplace canvas; Photoroom/Fotor gate colored/branded backgrounds AND sizing behind Pro or signup. So sellers still round-trip through Canva to recolor, composite, and resize. ListingCut does cutout → composite on white OR a custom hex color → at eBay/Etsy/Poshmark preset sizes with a margin → JPEG/batch-ZIP download in one click, with images never leaving the device.

Core flows:
1. Remove background client-side, one or many photos: user drags-and-drops or picks up to 20 JPEG/PNGs; the page runs the free @imgly/background-removal WASM model in the browser (no server upload, no paid API). Photos process sequentially in a plain vertical queue (thumbnail + filename + status per row); the first finished result auto-opens large with a side-by-side before/after. A failed row shows a red status with its own Retry button (re-runs just that photo, no reload); a nearly-empty cutout (alpha coverage below threshold) is marked "Check this one" in amber instead of Done and is excluded from the ZIP with a visible notice. The drop zone discloses BEFORE upload that the first photo downloads a one-time ~50 MB model; while it downloads and runs, a two-phase progress indicator shows download percent AND an estimated time remaining ("~12s left", computed from observed bytes/sec and counting down) then "removing background…" with elapsed seconds (in every path, including the sample) — never a frozen-looking percent with no ETA. A prominent, unmissable PRIVACY PROOF is shown on first open (not buried): a legible badge/banner stating all processing happens in your browser and the image never leaves your device, plus a self-verify affordance a skeptic can act on (e.g. "Works offline — turn on airplane mode and it still runs" and a note that the browser Network tab shows zero image uploads). The proof is a verifiable trust claim, not just passive copy.
2. Touch up + download transparent PNG: on any result, a "Touch up" button opens a canvas brush (Erase / Restore / Pan toggles, size slider, Undo, Done) to fix spots the auto-cutout missed; the brush is soft-edged (feathered), the canvas supports scroll-wheel and pinch zoom with drag-to-pan, and a preview background toggle (checker / white / dark) reveals edge halos; exports include the fix. "Download transparent PNG" is the secondary download, with a plain-language hint and the checkerboard captioned "Checkered area = transparent (no background)".
3. Export (the differentiator): presets are grouped "General & Social" FIRST (Square 1080×1080 — the neutral default, Story 1080×1920, Link/Ad 1200×627, Slide 16:9 1920×1080, Custom) then "Marketplaces" (eBay 1600×1600, Etsy 2000×2000, Poshmark square, Depop 1280×1280, Facebook 1200×1200). The default selected preset is Square 1080×1080 so a first-time visitor lands in a neutral size, not a seller size; marketplace presets remain fully available one tap away. A Background selector offers White (default), Color, Gradient, and Transparent modes. In Color mode preset swatches plus a native hex picker and #RRGGBB field choose the fill. In Gradient mode (disabled until a cutout exists; White stays the default mode) ~6 tasteful preset gradient swatches (soft gray, warm sunset, cool blue, mint, peach, slate) PLUS a custom two-color picker (two #RRGGBB fields each with a native `<input type="color">`) and an angle control (vertical / horizontal / diagonal presets, or a 0–360° slider) define a linear gradient drawn purely on-canvas via createLinearGradient (no model, no upload, no paid API) filling the WHOLE canvas behind the centered subject. The primary button's label reflects mode ("Download white JPEG", "Download JPEG on this background", "Download JPEG on this gradient", "Download transparent PNG") and updates synchronously with every chip/mode/swatch/hex/preset/angle change. A Margin slider (2–15%, default 6%, "space around the subject") controls the border; preset, background mode, chosen color, chosen gradient (preset-or-custom, its two colors, its angle), and margin are all sticky across photos and Start over. The gradient composes with the chosen preset SIZE and the Margin slider (subject centered, gradient fills the whole canvas behind it) and applies to BOTH single export AND "Download all (ZIP)" batch (every photo gets the same gradient). In White, Color, or Gradient mode an optional "Shadow" toggle (default OFF, with a small intensity/softness control at a sane default) composites a natural soft drop-shadow beneath the subject — generated purely on-canvas from the cutout alpha silhouette (offset + Gaussian feather + opacity), so it does not depend on fine-hair matte quality; it respects the chosen preset size and Margin, is sticky across photos and Start over exactly like background mode/color/margin, applies to BOTH single-export and "Download all (ZIP)" batch, renders OVER the gradient in Gradient mode exactly as it does over a solid color, and is disabled/hidden only in Transparent mode (available in White, Color, AND Gradient). "Download all (ZIP)" appears once 2+ are done.

The exported size still matches the chosen preset, and the centered subject still respects the Margin slider.

- GRADIENT — controls reveal: choosing Gradient mode reveals (mirroring how Color reveals its hex picker) ~6 preset gradient swatches, a custom two-color picker (two #RRGGBB fields + two native color pickers), and an angle control (vertical/horizontal/diagonal presets or a 0–360° slider); White/Color/Transparent are visually unchanged and White stays the default. The primary download button reads "Download JPEG on this gradient" while Gradient mode is active, updating synchronously when a preset swatch, custom color, or angle changes.
- GRADIENT — corner pixels equal the gradient ends: with Background = Gradient, a custom two-color gradient (e.g. #FF0000 → #0000FF) at a vertical angle (top→bottom), preset eBay 1600×1600, the exported file is exactly 1600×1600 .jpg and the corner pixels along the gradient axis equal the gradient's END colors (top corners ≈ (255,0,0), bottom corners ≈ (0,0,255), within a small tolerance) — NOT white and NOT a single flat color. Choosing a horizontal angle moves the two end colors to the left/right corner pairs instead. The centered subject still respects the Margin slider.
- GRADIENT — preset fills the canvas: selecting any built-in gradient preset (e.g. "warm sunset") and exporting at a chosen preset size produces a JPEG whose corner pixels are non-white and differ from each other (a real two-stop gradient, not a solid), at exactly the preset dimensions.
- GRADIENT — ORDER-SENSITIVE regression (writes the same shared export-settings object as color/margin/shadow): select Gradient, set custom colors #FF0000 → #0000FF and a fixed angle, THEN move the Margin slider — the gradient's two colors and angle must be UNCHANGED in the live preview and the next export (margin change must not re-initialize or clobber the gradient fields). Likewise toggling Shadow on/off in Gradient mode must NOT reset the gradient colors/angle, and switching Background Gradient→Color→Gradient must restore the SAME previously-chosen gradient (preset-or-custom, both colors, angle), not a default.
- GRADIENT — sticky across Start over and photos: pick a custom gradient (two colors + angle), click "Start over", and Gradient with the same two colors and angle is still the active selection (the next export's corner pixels still equal those gradient ends); the gradient also survives switching photos.
- GRADIENT — batch ZIP: with Background = Gradient and a chosen gradient, "Download all (ZIP)" bundles a gradient-filled JPEG for EVERY photo in the batch (each photo's corner pixels equal the gradient ends, none reverts to white or a flat color), at the selected preset size.
- GRADIENT — composes with Shadow: with Background = Gradient and Shadow ON, the exported JPEG shows the soft drop-shadow region adjacent to the subject rendered OVER the gradient (the corners still equal the gradient end colors, not white), confirming shadow composites over the gradient like over a solid color.
- A "Shadow" toggle appears inside the Export/Background panel, default OFF; it is disabled (or hidden) when Background = Transparent and enabled in White, Color, AND Gradient mode.
- With Background = White, preset eBay 1600×1600, Shadow ON, the exported 1600×1600 JPEG contains a visible darker (non-white) soft region directly adjacent to the subject while the four canvas CORNER pixels remain pure white (255,255,255) — i.e. the shadow is soft and contained, not a hard rectangle and not bleeding to the corners. With Shadow OFF the same export has no such darker region (it matches the legacy white export).
- The Shadow on/off state and its intensity setting survive "Start over" and switching photos exactly like background mode/color/margin: turn Shadow ON, click Start over, and the next export still shows the shadow region.
- With Shadow ON, "Download all (ZIP)" bundles JPEGs that all contain the darker soft shadow region adjacent to the subject (none reverts to a flat white background).
- Setting Background = Transparent and exporting at eBay 1600×1600 downloads a 1600×1600 .png whose corner pixels are transparent (alpha 0) and whose subject is centered at the same margin (distinct from core flow 2's original-size transparent PNG, which is not preset-sized).
- Privacy is observable: with browser devtools Network tab open, dropping and processing an image produces no network request whose payload contains the image — the only fetches are the page's static assets and the WASM/model files (GET requests, no image upload). Works fully after first load even if you go offline before dropping a second image (no server round-trip required for processing).

Out of scope:
- Background IMAGES / photographic or scene backdrops, and per-marketplace brand-color presets beyond the small built-in swatch set. Supported backgrounds are: solid white, custom hex color, two-color linear gradient (preset or custom, with an angle), and transparent. (Gradients are now IN scope; photographic/scene background images remain out of scope.)
- Adding photos to a queue that is already running (start over to begin a new batch).
- Accounts, history, saved images, server-side storage of any kind.
- WebP output, PWA/offline install, API endpoint.
- The drop-shadow is solid-soft only (a single feathered offset shadow with an intensity/softness control): no directional light angle/distance controls, no multiple light sources, and no reflections.
