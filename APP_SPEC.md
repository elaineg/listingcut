# ListingCut
Purpose: for anyone who needs a clean cutout — headshots/avatars, ads & social, slides & mockups, and marketplace listings — remove a photo's background entirely in the browser, then one-click export a JPEG on white, a brand color, or transparent at any preset size.
Problem: An Etsy/eBay/Poshmark seller prepping product listing photos, a job-seeker making a headshot, or anyone making a sticker/meme/slide needs a subject cut out of a photo — common one-off task, ~1–5 images per session, weekly-to-monthly for the typical user (daily only for professional sellers).
Beats alternative: Free client-side removers (NukeBG, bg.addy.ie) stop at a transparent PNG; the few that offer a colored background (addyosmani/bg-remove, Sticker Mule Trace) export at the photo's original size, not a marketplace canvas; Photoroom/Fotor gate colored/branded backgrounds AND sizing behind Pro or signup. So sellers still round-trip through Canva to recolor, composite, and resize. ListingCut does cutout → composite on white OR a custom hex color → at eBay/Etsy/Poshmark preset sizes with a margin → JPEG/batch-ZIP download in one click, with images never leaving the device.

Core flows:
1. Remove background client-side, one or many photos: user drags-and-drops or picks up to 20 JPEG/PNGs; the page runs the free @imgly/background-removal WASM model in the browser (no server upload, no paid API). Photos process sequentially in a plain vertical queue (thumbnail + filename + status per row); the first finished result auto-opens large with a side-by-side before/after. A failed row shows a red status with its own Retry button (re-runs just that photo, no reload); a nearly-empty cutout (alpha coverage below threshold) is marked "Check this one" in amber instead of Done and is excluded from the ZIP with a visible notice. The drop zone discloses BEFORE upload that the first photo downloads a one-time ~50 MB model; while it downloads and runs, a two-phase progress indicator shows download percent then "removing background…" with elapsed seconds (in every path, including the sample).
2. Touch up + download transparent PNG: on any result, a "Touch up" button opens a canvas brush (Erase / Restore / Pan toggles, size slider, Undo, Done) to fix spots the auto-cutout missed; the brush is soft-edged (feathered), the canvas supports scroll-wheel and pinch zoom with drag-to-pan, and a preview background toggle (checker / white / dark) reveals edge halos; exports include the fix. "Download transparent PNG" is the secondary download, with a plain-language hint and the checkerboard captioned "Checkered area = transparent (no background)".
3. Export (the differentiator): presets are grouped "General & Social" FIRST (Square 1080×1080 — the neutral default, Story 1080×1920, Link/Ad 1200×627, Slide 16:9 1920×1080, Custom) then "Marketplaces" (eBay 1600×1600, Etsy 2000×2000, Poshmark square, Depop 1280×1280, Facebook 1200×1200). The default selected preset is Square 1080×1080 so a first-time visitor lands in a neutral size, not a seller size; marketplace presets remain fully available one tap away. A Background selector offers White (default), Color, and Transparent modes. In Color mode preset swatches plus a native hex picker and #RRGGBB field choose the fill; the primary button's label reflects mode ("Download white JPEG", "Download JPEG on this background", "Download transparent PNG") and updates synchronously with every chip/mode/swatch/hex change. A Margin slider (2–15%, default 6%, "space around the subject") controls the border; preset, background mode, chosen color, and margin are all sticky across photos and Start over. "Download all (ZIP)" appears once 2+ are done.

Success checks (a stranger can verify in a browser):
- Page loads with a drop zone reading something like "Drag & drop photos (up to 20) or click to upload", a visible privacy claim stating the image never leaves the device / all processing happens in your browser, and a pre-upload disclosure that the first photo downloads a one-time ~50 MB tool — verifiable in <5 seconds without uploading anything.
- Preset buttons labeled "eBay 1600×1600", "Etsy 2000×2000", "Poshmark" (square), "Depop 1280×1280", "Facebook 1200×1200", and "Custom" are visible in the UI shell (disabled until a cutout exists) — verifiable without running inference.
- Dropping 2+ images shows a vertical queue with per-row status; rows finish one by one, each gets its own download, and a "Download all (ZIP)" button appears once 2+ are done and saves a .zip containing one white JPEG per photo at the selected preset size.
- On a finished result, "Touch up" opens an Erase/Restore brush over the cutout; erasing an area and pressing Done changes the subsequent PNG/JPEG exports in that area. The editor zooms with the scroll wheel (or pinch), pans via the Pan tool, and offers a checker/white/dark preview-background toggle.
- The use-case line leads with "Headshots & avatars · ads & social · slides & mockups · marketplace listings" (neutral, not seller-first). All five marketplaces (eBay, Etsy, Poshmark, Depop, Facebook) appear as preset chips; a note beside the Marketplaces group cites eBay's photo guidelines recommending a clean white background (no invented statistics). The default preset chip is Square 1080×1080, not eBay — selecting eBay must still work and export 1600×1600.
- A "Margin — space around the subject" slider (2–15%, default 6%) sits beside the preset chips; moving it visibly changes the white border in the live preview and the exported JPEG, and the value survives switching photos and "Start over".
- A row that fails with a fetch/processing error shows a Retry button that re-processes only that photo without a page reload.
- Dropping an image immediately shows a progress/status indicator (text or bar) that changes state (e.g. model download → processing → done) rather than a frozen page, even though first-run inference takes 5–20s.
- After processing completes, a side-by-side view shows the original image and the cutout (cutout rendered over a checkerboard or otherwise visibly transparent), and "Download PNG" saves a .png file whose corner pixels are transparent (alpha 0) for a photo with a plain background.
- Selecting "eBay 1600×1600" with Background = White and clicking the primary download button downloads a .jpg file that is exactly 1600×1600 pixels with white (255,255,255) corner pixels; Etsy export is exactly 2000×2000; Poshmark export is square (width equals height); Depop 1280×1280; Facebook 1200×1200; Custom matches the entered width×height. The preset choice survives "Start over".
- A Background selector offers White, Color, and Transparent modes (disabled until a cutout exists). Choosing Color reveals a hex color input plus preset swatches; the default mode is White and matches the legacy white export exactly.
- Setting Background = Color, entering hex #FF0000, choosing preset eBay 1600×1600, and clicking download produces a 1600×1600 .jpg whose corner pixels are exactly red (255,0,0); entering #000000 produces corner pixels (0,0,0). The exported size still matches the chosen preset, and the centered subject still respects the Margin slider.
- The chosen background mode AND color survive "Start over" and switching photos: pick #FF0000, click Start over, and Color/#FF0000 is still the active selection (corner pixels of the next export are still (255,0,0)).
- With Background = Color and a non-white color selected, "Download all (ZIP)" bundles JPEGs whose corner pixels are that color (not white) for every photo in the batch.
- Setting Background = Transparent and exporting at eBay 1600×1600 downloads a 1600×1600 .png whose corner pixels are transparent (alpha 0) and whose subject is centered at the same margin (distinct from core flow 2's original-size transparent PNG, which is not preset-sized).
- Privacy is observable: with browser devtools Network tab open, dropping and processing an image produces no network request whose payload contains the image — the only fetches are the page's static assets and the WASM/model files (GET requests, no image upload). Works fully after first load even if you go offline before dropping a second image (no server round-trip required for processing).

Out of scope:
- Background IMAGES / photographic or scene backdrops, gradients, and per-marketplace brand-color presets beyond the small built-in swatch set (solid white, custom hex color, and transparent only).
- Adding photos to a queue that is already running (start over to begin a new batch).
- Accounts, history, saved images, server-side storage of any kind.
- WebP output, PWA/offline install, API endpoint.
